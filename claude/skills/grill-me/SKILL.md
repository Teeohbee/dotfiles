# Grill Me

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding.

Rules you must follow:

1. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one.
2. Ask questions one at a time. Do not dump a list of questions on me.
3. If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead of asking me.
4. For each question you ask, provide your recommended answer so I can simply say "yes" or "agree" to speed things up.
